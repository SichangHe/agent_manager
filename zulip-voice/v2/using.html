Using Voice

Read your AI agents' messages aloud and talk back to them, from your phone. This page is for people who already have the app connected to their Zulip account. Setting up the server is described here.

Open the app
English · 中文

First minutes

  • Tap Connect and paste your zuliprc (Zulip: Settings, Account & privacy, Manage your API key, Download zuliprc). The key stays in this browser only.
  • Tap anywhere once after opening the app: iOS only allows sound after a tap.
  • Pick an agent with the chip at the top left. Its number shows unread messages in other agents.
  • Tap a message or a sentence to read from there. Tap again to pause. Auto reads new messages by itself.

The screen

  • Previous / next: one chunk back or forward; tap quickly to skip many.
  • Speed: - and + in steps of 0.1.
  • Mic button: dictate. It pulses amber while the microphone starts, turns red with a chime when you can talk. Check mark keeps the text, arrow sends at once, X discards.
  • Paperclip: attach a photo or file. It uploads to Zulip and its link goes into the message box; tap send.
  • First-unread button (dot beside a bar, next to the search button): scroll to the first unread message in this agent (does not play).
  • Next-unread button (appears when another agent has unread messages): jump to the next agent with unread messages, in the order of the agent list.
  • Back arrow: return to the agent you just left.
  • Mark read (small button on an unread message): clears the blue dot without playing it, and marks earlier messages in that agent read too.
  • Images and files show inline with their file name underneath, so you can tell which one an agent means. Hide folds one; tap its chip to show it again. Markdown tables are shown as tables and read cell by cell.
  • The agent list follows the priority order set by the human manager (lowest number first), then newest first.
  • Green, amber or red dot at the top right: connection. Tap it for the debug view.
  • Language: choose English or 中文 in Settings. Interface text and the app's own spoken prompts follow it. The voice keywords and commands stay English.

Hands-free voice control

Turn it on with the waveform button next to the mic (needs microphone access). The app listens on your phone: nothing is uploaded until you start a reply. Use headphones; speakers can feed back into the microphone.

SayWhat happens
attentionInput mode: reading pauses, a rising chime plays, and everything you say is typed as your reply.
tomatoEnds and sends your reply (a long silence also sends it). Reading resumes.
tornadoCancels and discards the reply or command, anywhere.
potatoCommand mode (from standby; not while you are dictating).

The four words can be changed in Settings. Anything you say outside a session is ignored. You can speak your reply in Chinese, but the keywords and commands must be said in English.

Commands (in command mode; the app says what it did):

  • next, back, again; next message, previous message; skip forward three; first unread (or unread)
  • restart / start / from the start: the current message again from its first sentence
  • stop / pause, play / resume
  • switch to NAME (fuzzy match; if several match it offers the top three: say first, second or third)
  • next agent (next agent with unread messages), switch back
  • send log (sends the debug log to the server)
  • tomato leaves command mode; tornado cancels

If none of your words is a command, the app says "Command not recognized" and shows the list. Command mode ends after 8 seconds of silence.

What the sounds mean

  • Rising two notes: ready, go ahead. Falling two notes: done or sent.
  • Double short beep: command mode. Single high tick: a command was done.
  • Two flat beeps: nothing was sent (nothing recognised, cancelled, duplicate, or empty session).
  • Four alternating tones: a send failed after retries. Three fast beeps: Zulip is rate limiting.
  • Falling four notes: playback was interrupted. Faint bell music after 5 seconds of waiting: audio is loading.

When something goes wrong

  • Tap the connection dot, then Send to server (or say send log), and tell whoever runs the service.
  • Self-test in the debug view checks ping, audio, speech, microphone and reconnecting in one tap.
  • Unsent messages and voice recordings survive reloads and bad connections and are retried.
  • iOS may discard the page in the background; reopening it restores your place.

Limits

  • The interface is English or Chinese (Settings, Language); the voice keywords and commands are English only. A personal client for one Zulip account.
  • Zulip carries all text and audio, so a rate limit (about 200 calls a minute) can slow things down; playback itself makes no calls.

语音使用说明

在手机上朗读你的 AI 智能体发来的消息，并对它们说话回复。本页面面向已经把应用连接到自己 Zulip 账号的人。服务器的搭建方法见这里。

打开应用
English · 中文

最初几分钟

  • 点按连接，粘贴你的 zuliprc（Zulip：设置、账号与隐私、管理你的 API 密钥、下载 zuliprc）。密钥只保存在此浏览器中。
  • 打开应用后先点按任意位置一次：iOS 只允许在点按之后出声。
  • 用左上角的标签选择智能体。标签上的数字显示其他智能体的未读消息数。
  • 点按一条消息或一句话，从那里开始朗读。再点按暂停。自动会自动朗读新消息。

界面

  • 上一条 / 下一条：后退或前进一段；快速连按可跳过多段。
  • 语速：- 和 +，每次 0.1。
  • 麦克风按钮：听写。麦克风启动时呈琥珀色闪动，可以说话时变红并响起提示音。对勾保留文字，箭头立即发送，叉号丢弃。
  • 回形针：添加照片或文件。它会上传到 Zulip，链接放入输入框；点按发送。
  • 第一条未读按钮（标题栏旁的圆点，位于搜索按钮旁）：滚动到此智能体的第一条未读消息（不播放）。
  • 下一个未读按钮（其他智能体有未读消息时出现）：按智能体列表的顺序跳到下一个有未读消息的智能体。
  • 返回箭头：回到你刚离开的智能体。
  • 标为已读（未读消息上的小按钮）：不播放就清除蓝点，同时把此智能体中更早的消息也标为已读。
  • 图片和文件内联显示，文件名在下方，这样你能分清智能体指的是哪一个。“隐藏”折叠一个；点按它的标签可再次显示。Markdown 表格以表格显示，并逐个单元格朗读。
  • 智能体列表遵循人类管理者设定的优先级顺序（数字小的在前），然后是最新的在前。
  • 右上角的绿色、琥珀色或红色圆点：连接状态。点按它进入调试视图。
  • 语言：在 设置 中选择 English 或 中文。界面文字和应用自己说的提示语都会随之切换。语音关键词和命令始终是英文。

免提语音控制

用麦克风旁边的波形按钮开启（需要麦克风权限）。应用在手机上监听：在你开始回复之前不会上传任何内容。请使用耳机；扬声器可能回授到麦克风。

说效果
attention输入模式：朗读暂停，响起一声上升的提示音，你说的一切都会被输入为回复。
tomato结束并发送回复（长时间安静也会发送）。朗读继续。
tornado在任何位置取消并丢弃回复或命令。
potato命令模式（从待机状态；听写期间无效）。

这四个词可以在设置中更改。会话之外你说的话都会被忽略。你可以用中文说回复，但这些关键词和命令必须说英文。

命令（在命令模式中；应用会说出它做了什么）：

  • next、back、again；next message、previous message；skip forward three；first unread（或 unread）
  • restart / start / from the start：从第一句重新播放当前消息
  • stop / pause、play / resume
  • switch to NAME（模糊匹配；如果有多个匹配，会给出前三个：说 first、second 或 third）
  • next agent（下一个有未读消息的智能体）、switch back
  • send log（把调试日志发送到服务器）
  • tomato 退出命令模式；tornado 取消

如果你说的词都不是命令，应用会说“没有识别到命令”并显示列表。命令模式在安静 8 秒后结束。

声音的含义

  • 上升的两个音：准备好了，请说。下降的两个音：完成或已发送。
  • 两声短促的嘀：命令模式。一声高音滴答：命令已执行。
  • 两声平的嘀：没有发送任何内容（没有识别到内容、已取消、重复，或空会话）。
  • 四个交替的音：重试后发送仍然失败。三声快速的嘀：Zulip 正在限制请求频率。
  • 下降的四个音：播放被中断。等待 5 秒后出现轻柔的钟声音乐：音频正在加载。

出问题时

  • 点按连接圆点，再点发送到服务器（或说 send log），然后告诉运行服务的人。
  • 调试视图中的自检一次点按即可检查连通性、音频、语音、麦克风和重新连接。
  • 未发送的消息和语音录音在重新加载和网络不好时都会保留，并会重试。
  • iOS 可能在后台丢弃页面；重新打开会恢复你的位置。

限制

  • 界面支持 English 和中文；语音关键词和命令只有英文。这是为单个 Zulip 账号设计的个人客户端。
  • Zulip 承载所有文字和音频，所以频率限制（约每分钟 200 次调用）可能让速度变慢；播放本身不产生调用。
